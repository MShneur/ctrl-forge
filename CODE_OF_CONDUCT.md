# Code of Conduct

CTRL-FORGE is built around disagreement without contempt.

## Expected

- Critique ideas, files, decisions, and evidence—not people's worth.
- Explain objections with enough detail to be useful.
- Admit uncertainty and correct mistakes.
- Respect privacy and do not expose another person's confidential material.
- Make room for newcomers and different levels of technical experience.

## Not accepted

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate humiliation, spam, or publishing private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock conversations, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

For sensitive conduct reports, contact the maintainer privately through the GitHub profile rather than posting details in a public issue.
